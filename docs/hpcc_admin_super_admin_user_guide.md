# Admin & Super Admin User Guide
Revision 2026.10.25.2 | 25 October 2026 | Howick-Pakuranga Chess Club

## Sign in, profile and passwords
Use the HPCC admin portal at https://hpcc-flax.vercel.app/club-admin/login. Your menu depends on your role. Sessions expire after 10 minutes of inactivity; typing, clicking and scrolling maintain activity. Expiry returns to login. My Profile lets you edit your name and phone. Email and role are read-only there. A password change requires the current password and a new password of 12-128 characters, followed by a fresh login. Super Admin manages authorised accounts and roles. Missing Auth profiles are flagged rather than automatically given administrator access.

## Statistical dashboard
Choose 7, 30 or 90 days and an optional tournament, then Apply filters. Views are Overview, Membership, Tournament Registrations, Events, Enquiries and Absences. Switch daily charts between bars and lines. Doughnuts show status counts; accessible daily tables accompany charts. New memberships count membership records, not renewals. Cumulative growth includes memberships before the selected period. Active members and enquiries awaiting action are current totals. Revenue uses paid receipts created in the selected period, not tentative registrations or guessed imported amounts.
Membership registrations and receipts remain separate from tournament registrations and receipts. Selecting a tournament filters its entries, receipts, events and matched absence reports; membership and enquiries remain club-wide. Upcoming events cover the next selected number of days. Completed events must have completed status. Absence matching uses exact normalised event titles. Unmatched or ambiguous names are flagged, never silently attached to a tournament. Distinct absent players are grouped using private identity data that is not sent to the chart browser.

## Payment account setup
Open Payment Accounts. Enter the HPCC account name, code, purpose and NZ account number, then Save. Only HPCC bank data belongs here. Canonical and legacy account-name/number aliases are detected independently. Set an active default for membership transfers and a separate active tournament default. One account may serve both purposes. Clear the old default before assigning a different one; multiple active defaults are rejected. A tournament-specific active account takes precedence. A referenced account must be deactivated rather than deleted. If setup fields cannot be saved, ask the technical administrator to apply the v4_8 migration.

## Membership options and bank registration
Membership Fees & Options controls public names, descriptions and prices. Only active options are shown on Join and renewal. New membership registrations offer Stripe or bank transfer. The server calculates prices from active options; a browser cannot supply the amount. For transfers, the final screen and acknowledgement email show the configured account name/number, exact amount and unique bank reference. Keep this reference. Instructions are snapshotted and are not replaced when the club later edits an account. An identical pending bank registration reuses the existing record and reference. A different existing membership needs club assistance or the renewal flow, not another new registration.

## Tournament setup and registration
Set the event type, dates, venue, rating, categories, fees, registration settings and bank account in Tournaments. Configure its cancellation/refund policy before inviting entries. Club events retain active-membership checks; general/open events retain their existing eligibility rules. Tournament fees remain separate from membership fees. All players are reminded to have current NZCF membership. New registrations require DOB, email, phone and an explicit photography Yes/No answer. Selecting No allows registration. A player under 18 requires guardian authorisation and a guardian name. Policy version, text, answer and acceptance time are privately retained. Do not place consent or contact details in public player exports.

## Tentative bank entries
A bank transfer is labelled Tentative - bank transfer until full receipt is checked. It does not reserve a place, appear in the paid confirmed-player list or count as paid revenue. The account, amount and reference shown when submitted are the instructions to use. Do not send a second transfer or create another entry if uncertain. Contact the club. Bank transfers are excluded from Stripe reminders and Stripe resume links.

## Confirm received payments
Only Admin and Super Admin can approve payments. In Members, the bank-payment action is available for pending bank transfers. Tournament approval additionally supports cash and other manually verified payments. Select Confirm payment, choose the method, enter a reference/note and explicitly acknowledge the full amount was received. Wait for success. The database writes the receipt, confirmed/active status and audit record together. The audit records approver, time, method, amount and note. Repeated approval creates no additional receipt. If Stripe is complete or processing, use Stripe verification instead; do not manually override it. An open unpaid checkout is safely expired before manual approval. Refunded/cancelled entries cannot be restored by approval.

## Payment reports and privacy
Select report filters and columns before exporting. Payment Method, Bank Reference, Last Payment Reminder and Payment Status Explanation are available in both membership and tournament reports. Bank-reference and personal fields are for authorised admin exports only. The public player page is a strict field allowlist with no DOB, email, phone, consent, bank reference or Stripe payment identifiers. Player/Vega preparation reports remain private. A sent email status means the sending provider accepted the request; it is not proof of opening or delivery.

## Scheduled Stripe reminders
The technical administrator must install the SQL, configure CRON_SECRET and set ENABLE_PAYMENT_REMINDERS=true after verifying HPCC email/Stripe settings. Eligible unpaid Stripe entries are checked against Stripe before a reminder. Paid, refunded, processing, cancelled, duplicate and bank-transfer entries are suppressed. First reminders are due after 24 hours, then every three days, with a final reminder during the last 24 hours before the cutoff. Entries younger than two hours and entries reminded within the last 24 hours are excluded. Full tournaments are skipped. A daily durable log prevents repeat attempts on the same day. Sending stops at the earlier of registration close and tournament start. The link resumes the existing registration, not a new entry. An expired unpaid checkout can be replaced under that existing entry after another payment check. A closed event or expired link cannot be paid through the resume flow.
Jobs are limited to 50 eligible attempts per run. The included Vercel schedule runs daily at 20:00 UTC. A technical administrator must arrange additional authenticated runs when volume requires it. Send failures remain logged; queued or uncertain attempts must be reviewed rather than blindly replayed. Never enable a real reminder run merely to test.

## Duplicates and refunds
Do not create or charge another entry when payment is uncertain. Concurrent matching submissions are protected by stable identities and database locking. Historical records are not deleted. Stripe full refunds cancel the linked tournament entry and retain its payment history; partial refunds do not automatically cancel it. Stripe's charge.refunded event must be enabled. For a prior refund, use the row's Stripe payment-check action to reconcile it. A completion replay must not restore a cancelled/refunded entry. No automatic refund is issued by the website changes.

## Content and photo albums
News, coaching, AGM and programme pages use editing and preview controls. In Interschool Chess or Poppies, choose Add new section, type the new content, add it to the editor, Preview page, then Save changes. Existing text and photos remain. Photos can be browsed, cropped, replaced and captioned. Poppies keeps editions by year. Annual Prize Giving has an import preview in Photo Gallery; importing copies photos to HPCC storage and creates editable records. Deploying an import button does not itself import anything. Only published photos appear publicly. Membership fee changes need the active option checkbox and a successful Save.

## Troubleshooting
If a Save fails, read the actual error; the button becomes available to retry. A missing SQL setup error needs the HPCC technical administrator. Default-account ambiguity needs one active default per purpose. If an entry is paid or processing in Stripe, use verification rather than approval or another payment. If a session expires, sign in again. If statistics cannot be loaded completely, the dashboard shows an error instead of partial totals. Check Email Diagnostics for sending failures. Preserve actual member IDs and payment history when resolving an issue; never use real refunds, deletes, charges or bulk sends as tests.


## Public View Entries and Excel
Enable Show public entries on the tournament. Public View Entries lists only paid and confirmed players. Registration order, Name A-Z, FIDE rating and NZCF rating sorting use the same order in Export to Excel. The styled XLSX contains sequence, FIDE title, player name, disclosed gender, FIDE ID, separate FIDE/NZCF ratings and club initials. The full club name is available on hover in the page. Valid FIDE IDs link to official profiles. Titles are fetched from FIDE, cached for a day and left blank on failure; an arena title is not treated as an over-the-board title. Email, phone, DOB, member IDs, consent and payment references are excluded. Blank information is never invented. Draft, archived or hidden player lists cannot be opened directly or exported. Public player pages are marked noindex. The separate private Player/Vega preparation report still includes the authorised personal fields.

## Gender and registration changes
Gender appears immediately after DOB, with Prefer not to say available. Membership and tournament forms validate required selections on the server. The technical administrator should apply migration_v4_9_tournament_gender.sql for the dedicated tournament field. Existing missing values remain blank. If the column is absent, the submitted answer is retained in the private consent snapshot; public displays remain blank until the field is configured and populated. No historical answer is guessed. The entry editor cannot change financial fields, tournament assignment or membership linkage.

## Preview reminders and open dashboard records
Admin/Super Admin can open Tournament Registrations, choose a tournament and select Preview due reminders. The preview checks setup and estimates candidates without sending email. Confirm and send is a real send action and is available only when HPCC reminders are enabled. Stripe verification, daily suppression, capacity and deadlines run again before each attempt. Read provider acceptance and failure counts; acceptance does not prove delivery or opening. Dashboard metric cards now open the corresponding member, entry, tournament, enquiry or absence records. Membership and enquiry links remain club-wide even when filtering a tournament.
